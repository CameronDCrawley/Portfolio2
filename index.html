About me

From social services to software, my work has always been about connecting people.

I spent years in NYC social services connecting individuals and families with the resources they needed. Building community was always at the center of that work.

After my daughter was born, I decided it was time to turn my interest in tech into a career. I joined 100Devs, where project-based learning and teamwork gave me a deep understanding of how the internet and the apps on our phones actually work. Through freelancing, I've built sites for nonprofits and local businesses and learned to take real pride in my work.

Now I'm bringing my two worlds together. My goal is to bridge the gap between disadvantaged communities and technology, and to teach others the skills people took the time to teach me.
